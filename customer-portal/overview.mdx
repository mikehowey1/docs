The Customer Portal is a branded, self-service dashboard that your hosting provider gives you access to. You can monitor every miner you have hosted at their facility, see your Bitcoin earnings in real time, view invoices, make payments, and open support tickets — without needing to contact your host for routine information.

## Accessing the portal

Your hosting provider will send you a login link. It may be a branded URL (for example, `acme.dunedigital.xyz` or `yourhostingco.com/portal`), or you can log in directly at [dunedigital.xyz](https://dunedigital.xyz) if your host uses the default domain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invite link">
    Click the link in the welcome email from your hosting provider. It takes you directly to the login screen for their portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Enter the email address and password your host set up for you, or use the magic-link option if your host enabled it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your dashboard">
    After signing in, you land on your home screen. If you have hosted equipment, you see a live count of operational miners. If you have O&M contracts, you see cards for each managed site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you have equipment hosted across more than one provider, each provider has its own portal login. You cannot merge accounts across hosting organizations.
</Note>

## Two portal modes

The portal adapts based on the type of contract your host has set up for you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Hosting portal">
    The **Hosting Portal** is for customers who have physical or virtual mining equipment hosted at a facility. You see:

    - A live equipment list with hashrate, power, status, and uptime for every assigned miner
    - Earnings dashboards showing gross and net Bitcoin per settlement period
    - Monthly invoices with power and hosting fees
    - HashGuard protection status and compensation credits
    - Alerts, contracts, marketplace access, and support tickets
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="O&M portal">
    The **Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Portal** is for customers who own a mining site and have contracted a host to operate it on their behalf. You see:

    - Site cards for each facility under management
    - Power performance dashboards: consumption, efficiency, and cost
    - Mining performance dashboards: fleet hashrate and uptime
    - Cooling performance dashboards: CDU status, coolant temperatures
    - A geographic map view of your sites
    - Automated O&M reports and invoices

    <Note>
      If your account has both hosted equipment and O&M contracts, the portal shows both. O&M site cards appear at the top of your home screen, and your hosted equipment is accessible from the **Equipment** section.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Home screen at a glance

When you log in, the home screen shows a real-time summary banner with the number of miners currently operational out of your total assigned count (for example, `14 / 15 operational`). Below that, quick-access cards surface the features most relevant to your contract type.

<Tip>
  The banner dismisses after you acknowledge it. To see the live operational count again, navigate to **Equipment** in the sidebar.
</Tip>

## AI-powered support chat

An AI assistant is available in the portal at all times. It can answer common questions about how billing works, explain hashrate terms, help you interpret earnings data, and route you to a support ticket if a question requires human review from your host.
