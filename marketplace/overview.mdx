The Dune Digital Marketplace is a global platform for buying and selling verified ASIC miners and hosting contracts. Sellers are approved before listing, every listing includes real performance data, and eligible listings carry HashGuard™ service-credit protection.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse listings">
    Visit [dunedigital.xyz/marketplace/home](https://dunedigital.xyz/marketplace/home) to browse all active listings. No account is required to browse — you can filter by equipment type, seller, condition, and HashGuard status before signing in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the listing detail">
    Open any listing to see full specs, live 24-hour performance data (average hashrate, uptime, efficiency), itemized fee breakdown, seller information, and HashGuard coverage status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to cart and check out">
    Add one or more listings to your cart. Checkout requires a Dune Digital account. You pay with crypto (via NowPayments) or Bitcoin directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfillment">
    After payment is confirmed, the seller fulfills your order. For hash contracts, your hashrate and monitoring dashboard are activated immediately. For physical equipment, shipping timelines are listed on the detail page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Public browsing vs. authenticated purchase

Anyone can browse the marketplace, view listing details, and filter results without an account. To add items to a cart or complete a purchase, you need a Dune Digital account. Creating an account is free.

<Note>
  To **sell** on the marketplace, you must submit a seller application and be approved. See [Selling](/marketplace/selling) for details.
</Note>

## HashGuard™ protection

HashGuard is an optional service-credit layer that some sellers enable on their listings. When your contracted hashrate drops below the seller-defined threshold due to an unplanned event, Dune Digital automatically calculates and issues a TH/s credit — valued at the day's hashprice — and applies it to your next invoice. No claim is required.

Look for the **HashGuard** badge on listing cards to find protected listings, or filter by HashGuard status at `/marketplace/home?hashguard=true`.
