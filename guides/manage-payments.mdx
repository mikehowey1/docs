## For hosting providers (Host OS)

### Generating invoices

Invoices are generated monthly for each active contract. You can trigger generation manually or run a batch for all customers at once.

**Manual generation — single customer:**

1. Navigate to **Host OS → Invoices** and click **Generate Invoices**.
2. Preview the invoice line items for the selected customer and billing period.
3. Click **Issue Invoice** to finalize and send.

**Batch generation — all customers:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open batch generation">
    On the Invoices page, click **Run Batch Generation**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the batch">
    In the dialog that appears:

    - **Billing Period** — select the calendar month to invoice (e.g., March 2025)
    - **Customer** — choose **All Customers** or a specific customer
    - **Skip customers with existing invoices** — keeps enabled to avoid generating duplicates for customers who already have an issued, pending, or paid invoice for the period
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review auto-send setting">
    The dialog shows whether auto-send is enabled for your organization:

    - **Auto-send on** — invoices are emailed to customers immediately on generation
    - **Auto-send off** — invoices are created as drafts; you review and send them manually

    To change this setting, go to **Host OS → Settings → Billing**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the batch">
    Click **Preview (1 Invoice)** to generate a single invoice first and review the output before committing the full batch. When you're satisfied, click **Generate All**.

    The batch runs in the background. The invoice list refreshes automatically when the job completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Invoice statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Not yet sent to the customer |
| **Issued** | Sent and awaiting payment |
| **Paid** | Payment received in full |
| **Overdue** | Past due date with no payment |
| **Held** | Customer is in default or has a billing hold — not emailed |
| **Written Off** | Debt forgiven |
| **Cancelled** | Invoice voided |

You can update the status of one or more invoices in bulk from the invoices table using the **Set Status** dropdown.

### Reviewing an invoice before sending

Click any invoice in the list to open the **Invoice Detail** dialog. Review:

- Line items and the billing method used (metered kWh, calculated, or hash-derived)
- kWh total and the variance percentage compared to PDU meter readings (shown color-coded: green < 5%, yellow < 20%, red ≥ 20%)
- Taxes, discounts, and credits applied

Edit any line items that need adjustment before sending.

### Accepting payment

Customers pay through the Customer Portal. The platform supports:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="ACH bank transfer" icon="building-2">
    No fee. Bank account linked via Plaid. Recommended for recurring monthly payments. Enable ACH on your organization in **Settings → Billing**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Credit card" icon="credit-card">
    Processing fee applies (passed to the customer or absorbed, depending on your settings). Processed via Stripe.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bitcoin" icon="bitcoin">
    Small network fee. Processed via NowPayments. Converted to your settlement currency automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="USDC / USDT" icon="banknote">
    Small network fee. Processed via NowPayments. Stablecoin payments settle to your account without conversion volatility.
  </Card>

### Setting up auto-pay for customers

Customers can enable auto-pay from their portal once they have a payment method on file. When auto-pay is active, outstanding invoices are charged automatically on the due date.

To check whether a specific customer has auto-pay configured, open the customer detail page and go to the **Billing** tab.

### Handling failed payments and delinquent accounts

When a payment fails:

1. The platform automatically retries the charge on a schedule.
2. If retries are exhausted, the invoice status moves to **Overdue**.
3. RevenueGuard flags the customer account. Future invoices are generated with **Held** status and are not emailed until the outstanding balance is resolved.

To manually resolve a delinquent account:
1. Navigate to the customer detail page → **Billing** tab.
2. Apply a credit, write off the balance, or mark the invoice as paid after collecting out-of-band.
3. Re-enable normal billing by clearing the hold in **Account Settings**.

### Viewing financials and payouts

Navigate to **Host OS → Financials** to see your financial position for any period.

Use the period selector to view **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly** summaries. The current period shows projected month-end estimates based on elapsed time.

The financials dashboard includes:

- **Revenue tab** — Gross revenue, net revenue, and a breakdown by billing method
- **Expenses tab** — Energy COGS (using per-site rates), platform fees, and discounts/credits applied
- **Receivables tab** — Aging schedule showing how much is current, 30-, 60-, and 90-days overdue
- **Tax tab** — Sales tax collected, use tax liability, and marketplace sales tax

You can add manual entries (one-time adjustments, repairs income, deposits) directly from the Financials page using **Add Manual Entry**.

## For hosted customers (Customer Portal)

### Viewing your invoice

Navigate to **Billing → Invoices** to see all invoices your hosting provider has issued to you.

Click any invoice to view:

- The billing period it covers
- Line items showing energy consumption (kWh) and rate ($/kWh)
- Taxes and any credits applied
- The total amount due and the due date

You can download a PDF copy of any invoice for your records.

### Paying with ACH (bank transfer)

ACH is the recommended payment method — it has no transaction fee and integrates directly with your bank account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Payments page">
    Navigate to **Payments** in the Customer Portal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link your bank account">
    In the **Bank Accounts** section, click **Link Bank Account**. This opens a Plaid connection window where you log in to your bank and authorize the link.

    <Note>
      Plaid uses read-only access to verify your account — it cannot initiate transactions without a separate authorization.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize the payment">
    Once your bank account is linked, open the invoice you want to pay and select **Pay with ACH**. Confirm the amount and authorize the debit.

    Standard ACH transfers settle in 1–3 business days. Same-day ACH is available with an additional fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Paying with card (Stripe)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Navigate to **Billing → Invoices** and open the invoice you want to pay.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select card payment">
    Click **Pay with Card**. You are redirected through a secure Stripe checkout page (hosted at `checkout.stripe.com`).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter card details and confirm">
    Enter your card number, expiry, and CVV. Click **Pay** to complete the transaction. You are returned to the portal once payment is confirmed.

    <Note>
      Card payments include a processing fee. The fee amount is shown before you confirm payment. There is a per-transaction maximum for card payments — for larger invoices, ACH or crypto is recommended.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Paying with crypto (Bitcoin and stablecoins)

Crypto payments are processed via NowPayments. The platform supports Bitcoin, USDC, and USDT.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invoice">
    Navigate to **Billing → Invoices** and open the invoice.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select crypto payment">
    Click **Pay with Crypto** and choose your preferred currency (Bitcoin, USDC, or USDT).

    The platform opens a secure checkout session (hosted at `pay.nowpayments.io`). You will see:
    - The exact amount due in the selected cryptocurrency (calculated at the current exchange rate)
    - A payment address and QR code to send funds to
    - A countdown timer for the exchange rate lock period
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send the payment">
    Send the exact amount shown to the provided address from your wallet before the rate lock expires.

    <Warning>
      Send the exact amount specified. Underpayments may not be credited automatically and require manual reconciliation by your hosting provider.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Bitcoin transactions typically confirm within 10–30 minutes depending on network fees. USDC and USDT on supported networks confirm faster. The invoice status updates to **Paid** once the blockchain confirms the transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Setting up auto-pay

Once you have a payment method on file, enable auto-pay so invoices are paid automatically on the due date.

1. Navigate to **Billing → Payment Settings**.
2. Select your preferred auto-pay method (ACH is recommended for zero transaction fees).
3. Toggle **Auto-pay** to enabled and confirm.

You will receive an email confirmation before each auto-pay charge is processed.

### Wallet top-up and balance

If your hosting provider has enabled wallet-based billing, you can maintain a pre-paid balance in your account. Invoices are deducted from your balance automatically.

To top up:
1. Navigate to **Billing → Wallet**.
2. Enter the amount and select a funding method (ACH, card, or crypto).
3. Confirm the deposit. Funds are credited to your balance immediately (ACH top-ups may take 1–3 business days to clear).

Your current wallet balance is shown at the top of the Billing page. You can set a low-balance notification threshold to be alerted when your balance falls below a set amount.
