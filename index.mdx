## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [dunedigital.xyz](https://dunedigital.xyz) and complete the onboarding wizard to choose your use case — fleet operations, hosting, or marketplace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install a site agent">
    Download and run the lightweight Hash Streams agent on your local network. The agent discovers miners automatically and streams telemetry to the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor and optimize">
    Use the Hash Streams dashboard to view live hashrate, power draw, temperatures, and earnings. Enable curtailment rules to cut costs during peak power pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grow your operation">
    Add Host OS to manage hosting customers at scale, list equipment on the Marketplace, or invite your customers to their self-service portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  New accounts start on the **Pay As You Go** plan with 200 MB of free data credits — no credit card required. Upgrade to **Business** anytime to unlock unlimited agents, advanced curtailment, and Host OS.
</Note>
